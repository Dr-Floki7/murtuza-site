## Direction contract

THESIS: One man changes clothes three times and the whole career reads without a word of
explanation. The surface owns the transformation itself, scrubbed frame by frame to the scroll.
It refuses the portfolio default: a static headshot, a stat band of big numbers, and a grid of
same-size cards summarising three jobs. The arc is not described, it is watched.

OWN-WORLD: Two grounds, one hard inversion. The pinned act runs on deep blue-shifted ink
(#0A0E14) lit like a dental operatory: one hard cool key on the subject, everything else falling
to shadow. When the act releases, the page inverts to cool paper (#ECEDEF) and stays there for
everything a recruiter actually has to read. Electric blue (#1B4DFF), flat and never gradient,
committed to whole regions rather than sprinkled as an accent. Archivo variable throughout: the
width axis does the work, expanded and heavy for display, normal regular for text, so one 86KB
file carries two voices. Hairline rules and a margin column of years carry structure instead of
cards. No eyebrow labels anywhere.

STORY: The visitor understands in about eight seconds that this candidate was a clinician before
he was a marketer, and that the clinical half is first-hand rather than researched. They believe
the healthtech pitch because they watched the sequence rather than read the claim. They download
the CV or send an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed scrubbed video on ink, subject centre-frame so a portrait phone crop
still holds him. Name set small and wide-tracked at top left. The display line sits lower left at
display scale, capped at 6rem, tracking no tighter than -0.04em. Primary action (Download CV) sits
bottom left directly under the display line, in flat electric blue, with the email path beside it
as a hairline-outlined sibling. A single scroll cue bottom right. Nothing centred, nothing floating
in a card.

FORM: User-pinned direction, which beats the concept roll by the skill's own rule, so no seed key
was drawn. The user specified the three-stage wardrobe sequence himself: white coat, then coat gone
with an unbranded handset, then handset pocketed at a lit architectural scale model. Ranked first on
resonance because it is the only direction where the motion and the content are the same object.
Two amendments accepted by the user in advance: no visible garment removal (transitions happen
between chained clips, never inside one, per the source PDF's own chaining technique), and no
recognisable phone branding.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
