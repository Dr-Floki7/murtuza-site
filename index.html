The obvious questions.

Q

Why did a dentist move into product marketing?

Clinical practice is a decision funnel. Patients weigh an expensive, irreversible choice, delay it, and eventually act. After a Bachelor of Dental Surgery and two years of general practice and community health camps, an MBA in Marketing turned that first-hand understanding of how people decide into a commercial skill set.

Q

Which sectors have you launched products in?

Three. Real estate, owning go-to-market for a slate of residential launches. Consumer electronics, at Xiaomi India, covering smartphone, smart TV and AIoT launches across an exclusive retail channel. Healthcare, through clinical practice and community outreach.

Q

Product marketing and go-to-market roles in healthcare, medtech and healthtech, where the clinical background and the launch experience are both useful. That combination is the point: most product marketers entering healthtech have the commercial half and have researched the clinical half.
