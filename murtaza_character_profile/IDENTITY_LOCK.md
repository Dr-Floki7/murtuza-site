# IDENTITY LOCK — MURTAZA

This file is the non-negotiable consistency layer for every image or video shot.

## Must remain consistent

- Same recognizable person
- Same broad facial proportions
- Same defined nose and nose profile
- Same dark eye and eyebrow region
- Same short dark textured hairstyle
- Same full dark beard and connected moustache
- Same fuller chin beard volume
- Same natural complexion
- Same general body proportions
- Same realistic, non-beautified appearance

## Allowed to change

- Outfit
- Location
- Background
- Lighting
- Time of day
- Camera lens and angle
- Pose
- Expression
- Action
- Props
- Cinematic color grade

## Forbidden identity drift

Avoid:
- Different person
- Generic handsome male face
- Clean-shaven face unless explicitly requested
- Completely different haircut
- Narrower or slimmer nose
- Altered eye color
- Excessively sharp jawline
- Plastic skin
- Over-smoothed skin
- Different beard length without approval
- Unnatural facial symmetry
- Ageing or de-ageing without instruction
- Unrequested glasses, hat, tattoos, scars, piercings or accessories
- Extra fingers, malformed hands or distorted anatomy
- Face partially hidden when identity is the main purpose of the shot

## Quality-control checklist

Before approving an output, compare:
1. Does the face look like Murtaza?
2. Is the nose profile preserved?
3. Is the beard silhouette correct?
4. Is the hairline and hairstyle consistent?
5. Are the eyes and eyebrows recognizable?
6. Is the face too beautified or slimmed?
7. Has the character accidentally become another person?
8. Are any changes intentional and documented as a variant?
