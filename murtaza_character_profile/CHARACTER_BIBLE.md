**Profile ID:** MURTAZA-001  
**Version:** 1.0

**Character type:** Realistic human digital character based on the user's own reference photos  
**Primary objective:** Preserve Murtaza's recognizable likeness across independent AI-generated images and video shots.

---

## 1. CORE IDENTITY

The character is a realistic representation of Murtaza based on supplied reference photographs.

The character should look like the same real person in every shot, even when the following change:

- Location
- Outfit
- Camera angle
- Lighting
- Pose
- Facial expression
- Activity
- Cinematic style

Do not replace the character with a generic model or an idealized version of the person.

---

## 2. PRIMARY VISIBLE APPEARANCE

### Face
- Broad, full facial appearance.
- Natural, realistic facial proportions.
- Defined nose bridge and clearly noticeable nose profile.
- Full lower face framed by a prominent beard.
- Preserve the actual relationship between forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, jaw and chin.
- Avoid sharpening, slimming, beautifying, or reshaping the face.

### Eyes
- Dark-looking eyes.
- Natural eye size and spacing.
- Preserve the shape of the eye and eyebrow region.
- Avoid exaggerated bright eyes, artificial catchlights, or altered eye color.

### Eyebrows
- Dark eyebrows with a natural shape.
- Keep the original spacing and thickness as closely as the reference allows.

### Nose
- Prominent, defined nose with a visible bridge.
- The side profile is an important identity anchor.
- Do not make the nose smaller, narrower, straighter, or more symmetrical than in the reference.

### Hair
- Dark/black hair.
- Short hairstyle.
- Natural texture and slight volume on top.
- Hair generally falls in a natural side-swept direction.
- Sides are shorter than the top.
- Avoid long hair, slicked-back hair, exaggerated fade, or a completely different haircut unless a separate variant is requested.

### Beard and moustache
- Full dark beard.
- Connected moustache and beard.
- Fuller growth around the chin.
- Noticeable beard volume along the jaw and sideburn area.
- The beard silhouette is one of the strongest identity markers.
- Do not randomly remove the beard, make it extremely short, or change it into a different beard style.

### Skin
- Natural medium-brown complexion as visibly represented in the supplied photos.
- Preserve realistic skin texture.
- Keep natural facial details rather than applying plastic skin, excessive smoothing, or beauty-filter effects.
- Match the reference lighting without changing the underlying complexion.

### Smile and expressions
- Natural broad smile with visible teeth.
- Neutral, thoughtful, serious, confident, and conversational expressions are allowed.
- Expressions may change, but the underlying facial structure must not.
- Do not exaggerate the smile or alter tooth arrangement.
